How to play

  • Drag back anywhere on the screen, aim, and let go. The further you pull, the harder the shot.
  • The dotted line shows where the ball will go, including bounces off walls. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Sink it first time for 3 stars. Stars earn coins, and coins buy ball skins and trails in the Shop.
  • Watch out for spinners, sliders, sand, ice, water, lava, conveyor belts, boost pads, portals and holes that move.

Ways to play

Worlds

Five worlds of 12 holes: Breezy Park, Dune Drive, Frostbite, Neon Night and Lava Works. Hole 12 of each world is a boss, where you collect keys to unlock the cup.

Daily

A new hole every day, the same for everyone. Play it each day to build a streak.

Endless

Holes keep coming until you run out of 5 balls. A hole in one earns a ball back.

Compete

Create a challenge of 5 holes and send the invite link to friends. Everyone plays the same holes whenever they like, and whoever takes the fewest shots wins. You can see who has accepted and who has finished, and your record counts wins out of games played. Each hole allows 6 tries; a hole you don't sink counts as 7.

Music

Pick music from the pause menu or Settings: the built-in Holeshot theme, tracks from Pixabay, any YouTube video or playlist, or Off to play your own. The speaker button mutes everything.

Put it on your home screen

  • iPhone and iPad: open the game in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open the game in Chrome, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

It then opens full screen like an app, and works offline (apart from Compete, YouTube and Pixabay tracks).
